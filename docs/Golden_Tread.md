
# Traceability Matrix (Golden Thread)

| Problem / Pain | Requirement (FR) | Solution / Design | Feature Built | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Users need to manage their personal information, and account securely, and basic services take time to process managing items. | **FR-1** Create, edit, delete account, and upgrade to Premium. | - User account entity<br>- Registration form<br>- Authentication<br>- Account management<br>- Premium Subscription | - Registration page<br>- Login page<br>- Account Settings | **TC-01:** Register, log in, edit account details and verify Premium access. |
| Users forget what groceries they have and may waste food or consume expired food. | **FR-2** Add, edit, and delete grocery items and mark them as used. | - Grocery item entity<br>- Expiration date field<br>- CRUD operations<br>- Item status (active/used) (Ch 5 & 7) | - Pantry Dashboard<br>- Add/Edit Grocery page | **TC-02:** Add, edit, delete and mark grocery items as used. |
| Users may find it difficult to remember or estimate expiration dates for different types of groceries. | **FR-3** Automatically calculate and display the estimated expiration date based on the grocery category and purchased date. | - Category-based shelf-life period<br>- Calculate expiration date using purchase date<br>- Allow date editing | - Add/Edit Grocery page | **TC-03:** Select a grocery category and purchase date; verify the estimated expiration date. |
| Users may forget about groceries until they expire, which can lead to food waste and possible health risks from consuming expired food. | **FR-4** Send expiration alerts/notifications. | - Expiration-date checking<br>- Reminder settings<br>- Notification service | - Reminders page<br>- Notification interface | **TC-04:** Check whether expiration reminders follow the configured reminder period. |
| Users may not remember what groceries they currently have at home. | **FR-5** Display all active grocery items with details. | - Query active items<br>- Display grocery name and expiration date<br>- Display item status | - Pantry Dashboard<br>- Active Items section | **TC-05:** Add grocery items and verify that all active items appear with correct details. |
| Users may overlook groceries that are about to expire or have already expired. | **FR-6** Show items that are expiring soon and already expired. | - Filter items by expiration date<br>- Identify expiring-soon items<br>- Identify expired items<br>- Sort by expiration date | - Pantry Dashboard<br>- Expiring Soon section<br>- Expired Items section | **TC-06:** Test different expiration dates and verify the correct item status. |
| Manually adding many grocery items after shopping can take too much time. | **FR-7** Premium receipt scanning. | - Camera scan<br>- Premium-only access check<br>- Review and confirm screen | - Premium Features page<br>- Scan Receipt page | **TC-07:** Scan a sample receipt and verify that detected items are displayed for confirmation. |
| Categorizing many grocery items individually can take additional time after shopping. | **FR-8** Automatic category detection. | - Automatic category detection<br>- Suggested category per item<br>- Confirm/edit category | - Scan Review page<br>- Add/Edit Grocery page | **TC-08:** Check whether detected grocery items receive appropriate category suggestions. |
| Different users may need different amounts of time to use their groceries before they expire. | **FR-9** Premium custom reminder settings. | - Reminder-period setting<br>- Saved user preference<br>- Notification service | - Premium Features page<br>- Reminder Settings page | **TC-09:** Change the reminder period and verify that the selected setting is saved. |
| Users may want to review previously expired groceries to understand their food-waste patterns. | **FR-10** Expired grocery history (Free: past 7 days, Premium: Unlimited). | - Expired-item history query<br>- 7-day limit for Free users<br>- Unlimited history for Premium users | - Grocery History page<br>- Premium History section | **TC-10:** Check expired-item history access for Free and Premium accounts. |
| Users may want to understand how much food they use on time and how much they waste. | **FR-11** Premium usage summary (daily, weekly and monthly). | - Usage statistics calculation<br>- Daily, weekly and monthly summaries<br>- Bar chart visualization<br>- Monthly count reset | - Usage Summary page<br>- Premium Features page | **TC-11:** Verify daily, weekly and monthly usage counts using sample grocery records. |
